# Reference fidelity review

Target: the supplied *The postbox* graphic-novel panel, not merely a functioning GLB.
The fixed-camera study must be reviewed before generalizing to a whole walkable scene.

## Required before calling the artwork complete

- Ari has a coherent anatomical silhouette, finished hair, recognizable face,
  cloth folds and a natural hand/letter pose without visible intersections.
- The mailbox has crafted surfaces and legible, correctly placed lettering.
- The composition reproduces the scene's storytelling hierarchy: character,
  letter and mailbox first; framing foliage second; distant city third.
- Foliage, masonry and distant architecture have convincing varied construction,
  not repeated primitive silhouettes.
- Materials and light support the vivid blue/coral/gold comic style and avoid a
  plastic render appearance, flat grey sky or excessive reflective flooring.
- Foreground, middle distance and cloud-filled background are clearly separated.
- Inspect the actual Blender render at full resolution; do not substitute an
  image-generated scene for that inspection.
- A playable delivery additionally needs baked materials, reasonable mesh/draw
  budgets, collision and interaction tests. Offline render quality alone is insufficient.
